Account and Data Deletion Policy

In full compliance with Google Play's User Data and Account Deletion Policy, users of The 20 Minute App (package: com.twentyminute.app) have the full right to delete their account and all associated personal and cloud data at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the app installed, you can permanently delete your account immediately:

  1. Open The 20 Minute App on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab (bottom navigation).
  3. Tap Account Settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the prompt. Your user record, cloud backup, and Firebase Authentication identity will be wiped instantly from our servers.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request (Without Reinstalling)

If you have uninstalled the app or no longer have access to your mobile device, you can request account deletion directly via email:

Send an email from the Google account associated with your 20 Minute App profile to:

delsigate@gmail.com

  • Email Subject: Account and Data Deletion Request - 20 Minute App
  • Information Required: The email address used to sign in to the app.

Our team will process your request, completely purge your cloud records from Firebase Firestore and Authentication, and send a confirmation within 48 to 72 hours.

What Data Is Deleted?

When an account is deleted, the following data is permanently purged:

  • User Profile: Name, email address, profile picture URL.
  • Authentication Identity: Firebase Authentication user credentials and UID.
  • Cloud Settings: Focus limits, sleep schedules, age settings, and custom quotes.
  • Focus History & Streaks: Real-time reel counter records, streak dates, and challenge stats.
  • Notification Tokens: Device FCM tokens associated with your account.

What Data Is Retained?

We retain zero personal data after your account is deleted. However, past in-app purchase and subscription transactions are recorded and managed by Google Play for financial and tax accounting purposes under Google's independent policies.

Cancelling Active Subscriptions

Important: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. To cancel recurring charges, open the Google Play Store → Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions → Cancel Subscription.
